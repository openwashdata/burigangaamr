## Data

Access the burigangaamr datasets

- [`burigangaamr_antibiotics`](https://openwashdata.github.io/burigangaamr/reference/burigangaamr_antibiotics.md)

- [`burigangaamr_isolates`](https://openwashdata.github.io/burigangaamr/reference/burigangaamr_isolates.md)
  : Resistance summary per Pseudomonas aeruginosa isolate
- [`burigangaamr`](https://openwashdata.github.io/burigangaamr/reference/burigangaamr.md)
  : Antibiotic susceptibility of Pseudomonas aeruginosa isolates from
  the Buriganga River
