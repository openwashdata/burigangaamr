# Titles and descriptions of the data objects

Drafts for the roxygen files. The same text is used for the hand built pass and for the pass with the washr skill.

## burigangaamr

Title: Antibiotic susceptibility of Pseudomonas aeruginosa isolates from the Buriganga River

Description: Zone diameters and interpretations of the Kirby-Bauer disc diffusion test for 50 Pseudomonas aeruginosa isolates from surface water at seven sites along the Buriganga River, Bangladesh, tested against nine antibiotics (450 observations). Tidied from the supplementary tables S3 and S4 of Sharif et al. (2024), doi:10.2166/wh.2024.270, CC BY 4.0.

## burigangaamr_isolates

Title: Resistance summary per Pseudomonas aeruginosa isolate

Description: One row per isolate with the counts of resistant and intermediate results, the number of antibiotic classes with resistance, the antibiotics listed as nonsusceptible in supplementary table S6 and the multidrug resistance flag of that table.

## burigangaamr_antibiotics

Title: Antibiotics tested and their zone diameter breakpoints

Description: The nine antibiotics of the disc diffusion test with their class, disc content and the zone diameter breakpoints used to interpret the results, from supplementary tables S1 and S2.
